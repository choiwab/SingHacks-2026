# Monday Brief

Monday Brief is the shared language for preparing a Relationship Manager to decide which clients need attention and how to conduct the next conversation.

## Language

**Monday Brief projection**:
The complete, review-ready view of priorities, pre-reads, evidence, and scenario rehearsals for one as-of date.
_Avoid_: App data, dashboard payload

**Priority**:
An ordered indication of how urgently a Client needs Relationship Manager attention, based on the size of a gap, its deadline, and its consequence. It is an aid to ordering work, not a suitability decision.
_Avoid_: Alert, recommendation

**Pre-read**:
A concise preparation package for one Client conversation, including changes, stated beliefs, relevant rules, a suggested opening, and known uncertainty.
_Avoid_: Client report, recommendation

**Evidence**:
A traceable source record or calculated fact that supports a statement shown to the Relationship Manager.
_Avoid_: Citation link, explanation text

**Scenario Rehearsal**:
A range-based examination of how a Portfolio could respond to a named hypothetical outcome. It supports conversation preparation and is not a forecast.
_Avoid_: Forecast, prediction, stress-test result

**Review Decision**:
The Relationship Manager's recorded approval, edit, or rejection of a suggested client opening.
_Avoid_: Feedback, action

**Client**:
A person, family, or organization whose wealth relationship and objectives are managed by a Relationship Manager.
_Avoid_: User, account

**Portfolio**:
A dated collection of holdings managed together under a mandate and reporting currency. A Client may have more than one Portfolio.
_Avoid_: Account, book

**Relationship Manager**:
The private-banking professional who prepares for Client conversations and retains responsibility for every Review Decision.
_Avoid_: Advisor, operator, end user
