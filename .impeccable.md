## Design Context

### Users

The primary user is Priscilla Ong, a private-banking Relationship Manager starting her Monday.
She needs to decide who deserves attention, prepare one trustworthy conversation, and record her review.
Hackathon judges must understand the full value loop in under three minutes.

### Brand Personality

The product should feel calm, exact, discreet, and editorial.
It should communicate private-banking judgment without resembling a trading terminal, analytics dashboard, or chatbot.

### Product Essence

The product has exactly three screens.

1. Monday list ranks all 20 clients and explains the score.
2. Pre-read presents changes, belief gaps, rules, money, an opening line, uncertainty, and source trails.
3. Scenario instantly switches between precomputed Strait reopening and escalation ranges.

Approve, Edit, or Reject is the only live write.
Every generated sentence links to structured facts or source records.
The narrator never receives raw CSV data.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a refined paper-led interface with warm ivory, charcoal, and one burgundy accent.
Use amber and green only for semantic urgency.
Pair Newsreader for editorial hierarchy with Instrument Sans for operational text.
Use structural rules and spacing instead of card grids, shadows, gradients, or glass.

### Interaction Principles

1. One primary task per screen.
2. No chat box, prompt box, simulated agent conversation, or decorative AI language.
3. Every consequential sentence has a visible **Why?** action.
4. Evidence opens in one right-side drawer with exact source rows.
5. Scenario values are ranges and are always marked as not forecasts.
6. Connector context explains where Priscilla left off without pretending to execute external actions.
